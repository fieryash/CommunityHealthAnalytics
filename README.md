# 🚀 Township Health Analytics: The Future of Community Wellness

**30,000 Residents. 10 Towers. One AI-Powered Health Revolution.**

---

## 🌟 The Vision
Imagine a township where your home doesn't just shelter you—it keeps you healthy. We've built a **hyper-local, data-driven health ecosystem** that transforms raw behavioral data into actionable wellness insights. This isn't just a dashboard; it's a **digital health companion** for an entire community.

## 💡 What We Built
We aggregated data from **Gate Logs (Food Delivery)**, **Gym Access (NFC)**, and **Wearables** to create a seamless feedback loop for residents.

### 🤖 The "Tough Love" AI Coach
Powered by **Google Gemini**, our AI doesn't just track steps—it holds you accountable.
- **Accountability Mode**: Missed the gym 3 days in a row? **We block your cheat meals.** Yes, really.
- **Dynamic Persona**: Shifts from a supportive friend to a drill sergeant based on your performance.

### 📊 Hyper-Local Tower Analytics
We turned health into a team sport.
- **Tower vs. Tower**: Real-time leaderboards ranking towers by aggregate health scores.
- **Incentives**: The healthiest tower wins **free maintenance** for a month. Gamification that pays off.

### 🥗 Smart Nutrition Integration
- **Partner Ecosystem**: Integrated with **Cult.fit, EatFit, and HealthifyMe**.
- **Intelligent Blocking**: Our algorithms analyze your metabolic health score. If it drops below 70, the junk food menu gets locked.

## 🛠️ Under the Hood
Built with a modern, scalable stack designed for rapid iteration and high performance.

- **Core**: Python 3.11 & Streamlit (for rapid, beautiful UI)
- **Intelligence**: Google GenAI (Gemini 2.0 Flash)
- **Analytics**: Pandas & NumPy for high-speed data aggregation
- **Visualization**: Plotly Express for interactive, responsive charts

## 🎯 Why This Matters
This platform demonstrates how **Agentic AI** and **IoT Data** can converge to solve real-world problems—fighting sedentary lifestyles and promoting community well-being. It's scalable, modular, and ready for the future of smart cities.
